## New analyses (2026-10-29)
- Production behaviour since 2026-10-07: Sol 299 reviews, 0.57 findings/review (72 blocker, 90 major, 7 minor) — reports very little. Opus 398 reviews, 1.21 findings/review but 233 minor + 145 nit (under-rates).
- Unused in-domain corpus: the orchestrator's review rounds since 10-07: 209 rounds on 136 PRs, 405 blocker/major findings (146 blocker, 259 major) on PRs the factory reviewer had approved. A generator exists (scripts/review-escape-cases.ts, #833) but the labelling pass has not been run. Past adjudication yield ~50% gold → ~200 more gold defects possible, which meets the ~160-200 defects power target.
- Escapes per PR did not visibly change with the reviewer model: ~2.0 orchestrator round-1 blocker/major findings per PR when the last factory review was Opus (79 PRs), 1.84 with Sol (50 PRs); ~3.0 across all rounds for both.
- Severity under-rating / follow-up parking: of the 405 orchestrator blocker/major findings, 26 (6.4%) heuristically match an earlier factory NON-blocking finding on the same PR (22 rated minor/nit, 23 of 26 by Opus; 4 majors parked as later-round follow-ups); 13 matched an earlier factory blocking finding.
- Grader audit (stored trials of E1, E3, D, dev + held-out): 651 required defect-trials, 264 caught, 387 missed; 192 of the misses had an uncredited blocking finding in the same file (173) or with high title overlap — a judge is adjudicating whether these are the same defect (result pending; if many are SAME, measured recall is biased low).
- How the gold is generated (the orchestrator's own review method): Claude (orchestrator, full context of issue, spec, prior rounds, recurring-defect memory) writes a PR-SPECIFIC ~1.5-2 KB brief — requirements restated as concrete checkable claims, every caller that must use the new check, specific test inputs, targeted questions ("does any caller still have its own inspection?", "do the tests fail without the round?") — then `codex exec` (gpt-6.1-sol, reasoning high, workspace-write: may add failing regression tests) executes it; plus a mutation check (revert src diff, PR tests must fail) and tests in two environments. The factory's review is one generic prompt at medium effort with no PR-specific brief and no mutation check. This is the most direct "replicate the oracle" lever.

## Grader audit result (2026-10-29)
An independent judge reviewed the 192 uncredited-candidate misses: 27 SAME (same defect reported at a root cause in another file, at the caller/catch site, or just past ±5 lines), 158 DIFFERENT (mostly real but separate defects), 7 UNCLEAR. 10 of 51 distinct defects had at least one SAME. Per system: production sol@medium 3 SAME on dev (33.7% → ~36.7% dev recall), adversarial prompt 8 on dev (45.9% → ~54.1%), union-of-2 D 6 on dev, held-out 2–3 each. So the ±5-line grader under-credits recall by ~3pp for production and ~8pp for the adversarial variant, biasing decisions toward conservative systems. Remedy: LLM-judge matching with location as a prefilter, validated on hand-adjudicated pairs; guard against one finding being credited to two golds.
